Arrow keys or WASD to move. Bump a slime to fight it with slope. Open chests for harder bonus questions and power-ups. Reach the portal to escape.

Escape the Slope Dungeon

Find your way through three dark mazes to the glowing portal. Level 1 gives you slope from graphs, level 2 from tables, and level 3 from two points.

Answer like 3/4, -2, 0, or undefined. Fractions must be in simplest form.

On level 1, points on the graph aren’t marked. Find spots where the line crosses exact grid corners, or tap Show points if you’re stuck.
